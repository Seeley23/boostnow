The website exposes the logo at https://boostnow.pl/images/logo.svg. The browser-rendered asset visually shows the complete white BoostNow wordmark and lime symbol on a black rectangular field, but svglib rendered only a partial “b” when embedded directly in the PDF. The print design must therefore use a rasterized/cropped browser-rendered logo or a separately reconstructed high-resolution mark, rather than direct svglib rendering of the source SVG.
